About this webinar
Retail refund processes often involve complex policies, customer information, order data, and multiple operational systems. An AI-powered refund support agent can automate these workflows, but production deployment requires more than intelligent responses—it needs strong governance, security, compliance, risk controls, and reliable system integration.

Join us at Krish Naik Academy for a hands-on technical webinar on Retail Refund Support FDE Project. Through an end-to-end AI Engineering project, learn how to build and integrate a refund support agent that can understand customer requests, work with enterprise systems, follow refund policies, and operate securely within a governed production environment.

Key Learning Objectives
Build an end-to-end Retail Refund Support AI Agent.
Integrate the agent with enterprise refund and support workflows.
Implement AI Governance, security, and compliance controls.
Manage operational and AI risks using guardrails, permissions, and validation.
Design monitoring and controls for reliable production operations.
Deploy the AI agent as a scalable, production-ready system.

AI Engineers, Generative AI Engineers, LLM Engineers, Agentic AI Developers, Software Engineers, MLOps Engineers, Data Engineers, Security Engineers, Cloud Engineers, and professionals building enterprise AI applications.

WHAT YOU'LL GAIN

Gain practical experience in developing and integrating a production-ready Retail Refund Support Agent with governance built into the system. By the end of this session, you'll understand how to combine AI engineering, enterprise integration, security, compliance, risk management, and production deployment to build reliable and governed AI applications.
