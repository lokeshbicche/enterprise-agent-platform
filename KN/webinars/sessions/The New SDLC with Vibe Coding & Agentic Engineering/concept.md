About this webinar
Software development is rapidly changing as AI coding agents move beyond code completion to participate across the entire Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). From understanding requirements and designing architecture to writing code, testing, deployment, and maintenance, AI is changing how engineering teams build software.

Join us at Krish Naik Academy for a practical exploration of the New SDLC with Vibe Coding & Agentic Engineering, with a focus on how AI Engineers and Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) can leverage AI coding agents to accelerate development while maintaining quality, reliability, and business alignment.

Key Learning Objectives
FDE & AI Engineering: Understand how AI is changing the responsibilities and workflows of modern engineers.
AI-Driven Requirements: Explore how coding agents can analyze requirements and translate them into actionable development tasks.
Architecture & Design: Learn how AI agents can assist with system design, technical decisions, and implementation planning.
Vibe Coding: Understand how developers can collaborate with AI agents to rapidly build software through natural-language instructions.
Agentic Development: Explore AI-driven coding, debugging, testing, and iterative development workflows.
AI-Powered SDLC: Understand how AI agents can support deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and continuous improvement.
WHO SHOULD ATTEND?

AI Engineers, Software Engineers, FDEs, GenAI Developers, Backend Developers, Full-Stack Developers, DevOps Engineers, and professionals exploring AI-powered software development.
