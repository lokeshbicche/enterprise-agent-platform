Launch Your Data Career
The AI For Everyone program is a comprehensive 3-month, hands-on program designed for beginners, professionals, and leaders across all domains: Engineers, Product Managers, Sales Professionals, Marketing Teams, HR Leaders, Operations Managers, Consultants, Entrepreneurs, and students. The course begins with foundational AI concepts explained through visual learning, then rapidly progresses to hands-on building of content systems, applications, automations, and AI-powered workflows using no-code tools.


This is not a "watch and learn" course. With an 80% hands-on focus, you will master the most powerful AI tools available today from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to automation platforms like n8n, Make, and Zapier, and no-code app builders like Lovable, Bolt, new, and v0 by Vercel. You'll build real systems, automate real workflows, and create real applications, all without writing a single line of code.


By the end of the program, you won't just understand AI, you'll be able to build AI-powered applications, automate complex business workflows, create professional content at scale, and lead AI adoption across your team or organisation with immediate workplace impact and competitive advantage.
 

Curriculum
What You Will Learn
Master the complete AI toolkit from foundational concepts and prompt engineering to content creation, data analysis, no-code app development, workflow automation, AI agents, and strategic AI adoption, all through hands-on building with 40+ industry-leading tools.

AI Foundations & Landscape
Understand how AI works, why it matters now, and how to start using it effectively in your professional workflows. Learn how AI systems work through simple visual explanations, including tokens, context, prompting, and how AI generates responses. Explore the leading AI models and tools, understand when to use each, and build your personal AI toolkit for everyday tasks.

AI Assistants Mastery
Master the most powerful AI assistants and learn how to apply them across real professional use cases. Leverage tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for writing, analysis, research, and day-to-day professional tasks. Learn how to communicate effectively with AI systems to produce high-quality, consistent outputs. Apply structured prompting methods such as role-based prompting, few-shot learning, and step-by-step reasoning to guide AI outputs. Create reusable prompt templates and structured AI playbooks for consistent performance across tasks and teams, including A/B testing for output optimization.

AI for Research & Productivity
Use AI tools to accelerate research, extract insights, and optimize daily productivity workflows. AI Presentations & Data Storytelling
Transform data and ideas into impactful presentations and business insights using AI tools. Create professional presentations and visual narratives from simple inputs using AI-powered tools like Gamma, Beautiful.ai, and Tome. AI Image Generation & Design
Create high-quality visual assets and brand designs using AI-powered image generation tools. Generate realistic visuals, product mockups, and marketing creatives using tools like Midjourney v7, Ideogram, and Adobe Firefly. Build scalable design workflows for consistent brand visuals across campaigns and platforms.

AI Video & Voice Production

No-Code AI App Development
